## Every low-confidence service

### 1. `2026-10-29-st-edmund-hall-0000` — St Edmund Hall, Thursday 29 October

> ```
> Thursday 29th October – Choral Evensong
> Sung by the St Edmund Consort
> Nunc dimittis: Mit Fried und Freud ich fahr dahin
> Motet: Ethel Smyth Komm süßer Tod
> ```

**Why low:** the card calls it "Choral Evensong", but it sits in the Thursday slot
and carries Compline's music — a Nunc dimittis and a motet — exactly like every
other Thursday entry on the card. The label and the content disagree. The printed
type is kept and **no time is recorded**: the card's header gives 6:15pm for Sunday
Evensong and 9:30pm for Thursday Compline, and neither clearly applies.

### 2. `2026-10-30-lady-margaret-hall-1800` — LMH, 3rd Week Friday

> ```
> Week 3
> Thursday 29 October
> 12 p.m.        Midday Prayer
> Friday 30 November – Towards All Saints and All Souls
> ```

**Why low:** the entry is headed **"Friday 30 November"** but printed under
"Week 3", immediately after Thursday 29 October — and 30 November 2026 is a
Monday. Dated from the week heading (Friday of 3rd Week = **30 October**) and
flagged. Time from the list's header note.

## Needs a human

1. **Christ Church has only one month of three.** The cathedral publishes monthly.
   The October list (28 September – 1 November) is out; November and December are
   not. **2 November – 5 December is empty for the cathedral** and needs a re-run
   once those lists appear. This is the single biggest gap in the term.

2. **Roman Catholic Masses — a scope decision I changed.** Earlier runs omitted RC
   Masses in chapels as out of scope. Two this term are *choir-sung with printed
   repertoire*, so I recorded them and am flagging it rather than deciding quietly:
   - Worcester, Tue 27 October 18:00, "Roman Catholic Mass sung by the Lower
     Voices", Setting `Mass XI`, two motets.
   - St Edmund Hall, Mon 30 November 18:00, "Roman Catholic Mass", Setting
     `Palestrina Missa Aeterna Christi munera`.

   I left out the RC Masses that print no choir and no music (LMH Wed 25 November,
   Merton Fri 13 November, Balliol Wed 2 December, St Peter's Fri 6 November — the
   last explicitly "A said service"). If you'd rather keep the old blanket
   exclusion, those two records should come out.

3. **Services the choirs sing away from their own chapel — not recorded.** Four
   lists print them; a service in London is not a service in an Oxford chapel:
   - Trinity: Carol Service at All Saints, Margaret Street, London, Wed 2 December.
   - St Peter's: Christmas Carol Concert, All Saints, Margaret Street, Fri 11 December.
   - Magdalen: Alumni Carol Service, All Saints, Margaret Street, Thu 10 December.
   - Merton: "A Ceremony of Carols" at Smith Square, London, Mon 14 December
     (also a concert, not a service).

4. **One service appears under two venues, correctly.** The joint Evensong of
   1 October, 18:15 — Merton's Choristers with Magdalen's, **held in Merton
   College Chapel** — is printed in both colleges' lists, so it is recorded under
   both `magdalen` and `merton`, each from its own source. The Magdalen record
   carries the note "This service will take place in Merton College Chapel." The
   site will show it twice, once per chapel.

5. **Outdoor carol events — not recorded**, consistent with the May Morning
   precedent: LMH "Carols in the Quad" (Wed 2 December) and St Edmund Hall
   "Carols in the Quad" (Fri 4 December).

6. **Two source misprints kept verbatim** (Wadham, 22 and 29 November):
   `Walton, Seat me as seal` (the work is "Set me as a seal upon thine heart") and
   `Tabakova, Good-will to mean and peace on earth` ("…to men…"). Both flagged in
   `parserNote`.

7. **Two validator warnings that are honest, not errors.**
   `venueStatus.worcester`: 33 services where the registry pattern suggests ~9/week
   — the Monday "Choral Reflection" in `typicalPattern` is not printed on this
   term's list at all. `venueStatus.lincoln`: 11 services against ~3/week — Lincoln
   prints one Sunday Evensong a week plus four midweek services, so the registry's
   pattern is overstated. Neither is a parse failure; `typicalPattern` is what
   drifts.

8. **Lincoln dropped its headings.** Earlier terms labelled `Psalm:`,
   `Canticles:`, `Anthem:`. This term's list labels only the responses/preces and
   the psalm, so canticles, anthem, hymns and voluntary are assigned by position —
   which is why 10 of Lincoln's 11 services are `medium`.

9. **Worcester is readable for the first time.** Its "Services and Music" PDF has
   a usable text layer this term (495 chars/page over 16 pages); previous runs had
   to mark it `not-parsed` as an image-only PDF. 33 services recovered.

10. **Oriel still publishes no music.** As in Trinity 2026, the term card gives
    themes, occasions and preachers and points to "the Chapel web page" for the
    music. Neither `/chapel` nor `/chapel/choir-and-music/` carries a list, so
    Oriel's 11 services are all `not-yet-published`.
