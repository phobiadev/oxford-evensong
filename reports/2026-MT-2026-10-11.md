Mid-term re-run, on the Sunday of 1st Week, four days after the first parse
(`reports/2026-MT-2026-10-07.md`). That run was made in 0th Week, when 12 of the 26
publishing venues had nothing up yet; the point of this one was to pick up the lists
that have appeared since and to check the 14 already in the file for amendments.

**Outcome.** All 26 publishing venues were re-checked. Three venues are new:

- **New College** has published its 21-page Michaelmas music list — **54 services**,
  the largest single addition this term.
- **Jesus College** has published its music list — **9 services**.
- **Exeter College** has *not* published its music list ("will be available here
  shortly"), but has published a Michaelmas **term card** which, unlike previous

The other 9 outstanding venues are unchanged, and each one's `venueStatus.note` now
records what was checked today and what was found. The 14 lists already in the file
were all re-downloaded and every one is **byte-identical** to its committed copy, so
no service record in them changed and none of their `fetchedAt` stamps moved.

Total: **402 services** across **17 venues with services** (was 324 across 14).

### Why the 14 were not re-parsed

Every published venue's page was re-scraped first: all 14 still link the same file at
the same URL as on 2026-10-07 (Magdalen's `UPDATED-11-SEP`, Lincoln's
`Music-List-MT26.pdf`, Pusey House's first `_files/ugd/…` link under the Michaelmas
heading, and so on). Each file was then re-downloaded and compared by SHA-256 against
`sources/2026-MT/`: all 14 matched, including the University Church's HTML page. Per
the skill's idempotence rule their `source.fetchedAt` values are left as they were, so
`git diff` shows nothing for them.

## New this run

### New College — 54 services, weeks 0–11

The list is on the choir's own site, `newcollegechoir.com/choralservices`, as
"26c Michaelmas Music list" (`download.asp?file=205&type=pdf`); the college page at
`new.ox.ac.uk/choir-and-chapel`, which said "Music List (forthcoming)" on 2026-10-07,
now points at the same file. 21 pages, text layer 532 chars/page, and clean: the en
dashes, `æ` in *Suscipe quæso Domine*, and the accents in *Dupré*, *Du Mage*,
*de Séverac*, *Escaich* and *Évocation* all extract correctly (checked by character
census — no mangling of the kind Magdalen and Merton have had).

Services run from Friday 9 October (0th Week, before Full Term) to Christmas Eve.
Six services a week — Sunday 5.45pm, Monday 6.15pm sung by the clerks, Tuesday,
Thursday and Friday 6.15pm, Saturday 5.45pm — plus Compline on the Tuesdays of 2nd and
5th Week and Vespers sung by the choristers on three Thursdays.

Decisions worth recording:

- **Every service is `medium`** (one is `low`, below). The list labels only the
  responses and psalms; canticles, anthem, organ pieces and Mass settings are assigned
  by position, as in Trinity 2026, and each record carries the standing New College
  `parserNote` explaining the mapping.
- **Wednesdays print "No choral service"** and produce **no record** — 7 of them.
- **Dropped from `text`**: the superscript `NC` that marks a New College composer, the
  `NCH nn` hymn-book numbers, and the organ-scholar initials `BT` / `HR` / `JH`. The
  initials turn out to be a useful signal: they appear only against organ pieces, which
  is how the opening `other`/Organ and closing `voluntary` items were identified.
- **Not recorded**: the Monday 8.30pm recitals (the list's own front matter calls them
  performances, not services), and the Bach *Christmas Oratorio* on 14 December, which
  is a ticketed concert **at the University Church** — an away event, so out of scope.
- **Vespers antiphons.** The three Thursday Vespers print the antiphons in a second
  column beside the hymn, psalm and Magnificat. They are recorded as `other` with
  `label: "Antiphon"`, interleaved in printed row order, and the `parserNote` says so.
- **The termly Cantata** (22 November, *Cantata Vespers*) is recorded as `other` with
  `label: "Cantata"`, which is the list's own word for it in the front matter.
- **One visiting choir**: 26 November, "Evensong sung jointly with Ely College" →
  `choir` set. The Clerks, the choristers and the Collegium Novum Consort that sings
  Midnight Mass are all the foundation's own, so `choir` stays null for them.
- **Four services fall after the end of Full Term** (Carol Service 6 December, Family
  Carol Service 9 December, Midnight Mass 24 December) and are recorded as the list
  prints them. 24 December trips the validator's "outside 0th–9th Week" warning; the
  site clips to the term window, so none of them will show.

### Exeter College — 15 services from a term card, no music

This one involved a judgment call, flagged here for your eye.

The chapel page still says **"The Michaelmas Term Music List will be available here
shortly"**, so there is no music. But the page now offers
`Chapel-Michaelmas-Term-2026.pdf`, and where Exeter's term cards have previously been
image-only, this one extracts at ~1,490 chars/page. It carries eight dated Sunday
evening services with their preachers, two dated "Special Occasions" and five dated
Advent and Christmas services.

Following the precedent set for Oriel and St John's, those 15 dated services are
recorded with `musicStatus: "not-yet-published"` and `music: []`. What is **not**
recorded is the card's weekly *pattern* block — Tuesday and Friday 6.15pm Choral
Evensong, Thursday 9pm Compline in Weeks 2, 4 and 6 — because it gives no dates; same
rule as Oriel's Wednesday Communion last run.

**I left the venue status at `not-yet-published`, not `published`.** Oriel is
`published` on the strength of a term card, but Oriel publishes nothing else, whereas
Exeter has a music list explicitly still to come. Keeping it `not-yet-published` means
the site's arrival notice still, correctly, names Exeter as a chapel whose list is not
out — while the services themselves are now visible. Say the word if you would rather
it read `published`.

Also not recorded: the 11am **Family Nativity Play** on 5 December (a chapel event, not
a sung service), said Mattins, the OICCU prayers and the Tuesday lunchtime organ
recitals. The 29 November entry is printed twice on the card — as "Advent Sunday" in
the Sunday list and as "The Advent Procession" at 6pm under Advent and Christmas — and
is recorded **once**, with both printings named in the locator.

A typo is preserved verbatim, as the rules require: the 18 October note reads
"St Alban's **Episocpal** Church in Washington DC".
