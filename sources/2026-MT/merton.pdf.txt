                  Merton College Chapel

For more than seven centuries, worship has been offered in this
place. Merton College was founded by Walter de Merton in 1264,
and work on the Church of St Mary and St John was begun in the
late 1280s. Today, the Chapel stands as part of the Church of
England, itself part of the worldwide Anglican Communion. The
Archbishop of Canterbury is the College’s Visitor. Here the
sacraments are celebrated, the daily offices are offered, and the
praises of God are sustained by the College Choirs. A lively and
diverse community, drawn from within the College and beyond,
continues this faithful offering of worship with daily services
during term, to which all visitors are warmly invited.

The Choral Foundation was established at Merton in 2008. The
College Choir sings on Sundays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays, while
the Girl Choristers, added to the Foundation in 2016 and with
members drawn from local schools, normally sing Vespers on
Mondays and Evensong on Wednesdays. The Dobson pipe organ,
installed in 2013, supports this rich musical tradition. Recordings
by the College Choir, the Girl Choristers, and organ recitalists are
available from the Porters’ Lodge and through the College
website.

The Chapel supports a number of charitable causes. This term,
collections will be given to Sing Inside, a charity which brings
inclusive, high-quality singing workshops into prisons, helping
participants develop skills, confidence and enjoyment while
building positive connections with volunteers from the wider
community.
