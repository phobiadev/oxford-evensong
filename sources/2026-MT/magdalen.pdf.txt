We welcome you to our worshipping community at Magdalen. Prayer and praise have
been offered to God in this place since the fifteenth century, when the College was
founded by William of Waynflete, Bishop of Winchester. The statutes of 1480 make
provision for a Choir comprising sixteen boy Choristers and a number of adult Clerks,
‘that the worship of God, from whom all good things come, might be increased more
widely and better sustained’. That tradition continues unchanged today. The
choirmaster at Magdalen retains the ancient Latin title Informator Choristarum, and
among those who have held the post over the centuries are John Sheppard, Daniel
Purcell, Sir John Stainer, Sir William McKie, and, in more recent years, Dr Bernard
Rose. Former choristers include the composer Ivor Novello; the late comedian and
actor, Dudley Moore, was Organ Scholar here, and a number of former Academical
Clerks hold prominent positions in the musical, legal and media worlds.
We strive to dedicate all our services here to the glory of God. Our music is not an
end in itself but part of a larger whole. We seek to inspire, to nourish, to console, and
also to challenge. We hope that the music sung in this Chapel represents not only
something of the extraordinary heritage of which we are the current proud custodians
– through pieces written by former or current Clerks, Choristers, Informatores and
Organ Scholars, indicated by the symbol M next to the name of a composer – but also
the richness and diversity of sacred music from across the Christian world, particularly
in the last half-century, when more music by women composers and lesser-known
works from the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries have entered the repertoire. Living
composers are indicated by the presence of their first name in addition to their
surname.
We hope that you will return to this place regularly, but, even if you happen upon
just one day in the life of a Chapel and Choir that has sung daily services for over five
centuries, we hope that, in the singing and in the silence, in the spoken word and in
the beauty of this house of God, you will feel that greater presence, and be blessed by
the peace that passes all understanding.

                                      Choral Services
Services are normally sung by the College Choir made up of Choristers, educated at
Magdalen College School, and Academical Clerks, who are ordinarily students at the
College. Evensong on Saturdays is normally sung by the Consort of Voices, in which
sopranos join the Academical Clerks. Tuesdays are usually sung by the Choristers
alone, and Fridays by the Academical Clerks alone. Compline on Tuesdays is usually
sung by two cantors drawn from the Academical Clerks and Consort of Voices. Details
are given in the Music List when services depart from this pattern, such as during the
school half-term holiday (23rd to 30th October). At Festal Evensongs marking saints’
days and major feasts of the Church’s year, a seasonal antiphon is sung. On Sundays,
at the Eucharist, there is a short homily of approximately five minutes, and at Evensong
(in some weeks) a sermon of approximately ten minutes.

                                        Organ Music
Organ music plays an integral part in our liturgy. The Assistant Organist, Drew Sellis,
and Organ Scholars, Sea-am Thompson and Sebastian Moreau, play for the majority of
the services in Chapel, assisted occasionally by the Informator Choristarum &
Organist, Mark Williams, who ordinarily directs the Choir. Details of the organ, built
in 2022 by Hermann Eule Orgelbau, can be found on the College website, and details
of recitals are given on the penultimate page of this booklet.

                                   Retiring Collections
Our retiring collections will go to support the work of the Chapel’s chosen charity for
the term, Crisis Skylight Oxford, which works with those who are homeless or at
risk of homelessness to find a home, find work, and to improve health and wellbeing.
www.crisis.org.uk/get-help/oxford/
