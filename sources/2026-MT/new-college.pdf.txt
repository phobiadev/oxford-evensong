NOT TO BE REMOVED FROM THE CHAPEL
          ELCOME to New College Chapel, where worship has been offered

W         since the late fourteenth century. We hope you will find peace and space
          for reflection in this beautiful building, built to the glory of God and
intended to resound with music and words of praise.
The term’s music covers a wide chronological and stylistic range, from plainchant
to music composed this year. On Friday 16 October we mark the release of New
College Choir’s recording of music by Tallis by singing his great penitential motet
Suscipe quaeso Domine. The termly Cantata is, as has become traditional in
Michaelmas Term, Bach’s much-loved Wachet auf! ruft uns die Stimme. Composers
with a link to the college are identified by the superscript initials NC. Building
work on the vestries this year prevents daytime recitals, but there are
performances every Monday evening.
The Revd Dr Erica Longfellow                                       Robert Quinney
