WELCOME
The Choir of St Peter’s College sings Choral Evensong in the College Chapel
twice a week during Full Term. All are welcome, whatever your beliefs.

Evensong is at 6pm on Sundays and 6.15pm on Thursdays unless otherwise
shown, and lasts about 45 minutes. Services are led by the Chaplain unless
otherwise indicated.

Morning Prayer is said on Mondays and Fridays at 7.45am and lasts about 20
minutes.

Lunchtime concerts take place every Thursday at 1.15pm, usually in the Chapel.
When a concert is in the Music Room instead, there will be a sign at the College
entrance.

Thursday services and the Sunday Carol Service are live-streamed on the
College Chapel YouTube channel: youtube.com/@spcoxfordchapel

Hymn numbers refer to Hymns Ancient & Modern (the red hymn book).
