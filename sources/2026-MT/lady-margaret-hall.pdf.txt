Prayer for Lady Margaret Hall
Vouchsafe, O Lord, to prosper with Thy blessing the work of this Hall, dedicated to Thy glory
and the good of Thy people : grant to all who teach and all who are taught, being enlightened by
Thy truth, preserved by Thy fear, and abounding in charity, they may daily advance in wholesome
learning and in the knowledge and likeness of Him who is the Way, the Truth, and the Life ;
to whom with Thee and the Holy Ghost, Three Persons and One God, be all honour and glory,
now and for evermore. Amen.
