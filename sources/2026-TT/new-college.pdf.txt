NOT TO BE REMOVED FROM THE CHAPEL
         ELCOME to New College Chapel, where worship has been offered

W       since the late fourteenth century. We hope you will find peace and space
        for reflection in this beautiful building, built to the glory of God and
intended to resound with music and words of praise.
This term’s music spans five centuries – not including the plainchant repertory,
which of course has its origins in an older (and originally aural) tradition. We
welcome a number of visiting singers, among them the choirs of Winchester and
Eton Colleges for a joint service celebrating the ‘Amicabilis Concordia’ agreed
between the institutions founded in the fourteenth century by William of
Wykeham, and a few decades later by King Henry VI. In our termly Cantata
Vespers we perform the extraordinary Sie werden euch in den Bann tun (literally
‘they will ban you [from the synagogues]’, as Jesus told his disciples in the so-
called ‘Farewell Discourse’ toward the end of the Gospel of John, and which
formed the basis of Bach’s 1725 Eastertide cycle of cantatas to texts by Mariane
von Ziegler. At the final service of the academic year we give the first
performance of A new song, a setting of the fourteenth century mystic Richard
Rolle by current Academical Clerk Zachary Roberts. Throughout the music list,
former or current New College composers are identified by the superscript
initials NC.
The brief night office of Compline is sung in 2nd and 5th Weeks. Weekly organ
recitals take place on Thursday lunchtimes, and there are chamber music recitals
on Monday of 6th and 7th Weeks at 8.30 pm.
The annual Chorister Open Day takes place on Saturday 6 June from 4.00 pm:
please contact choir@new.ox.ac.uk for further information and to book a place.
