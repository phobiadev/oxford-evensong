We welcome you to our worshipping community at Magdalen. Prayer and praise have
been offered to God in this place since the fifteenth century, when the College was
founded by William of Waynflete, Bishop of Winchester. The statutes of 1480 make
provision for a Choir comprising sixteen boy Choristers and a number of adult Clerks,
Ôthat the worship of God, from whom all good things come, might be increased more
widely and better sustainedÕ. That tradition continues unchanged today. The
Choirmaster at Magdalen retains the ancient Latin title Informator Choristarum, and
among those who have held the post over the centuries are John Sheppard, Daniel
Purcell, Sir John Stainer, Sir William McKie, and, in more recent years, Dr Bernard
Rose. Former choristers include the composer Ivor Novello; the late comedian and
actor, Dudley Moore, was Organ Scholar here, and a number of former Academical
Clerks hold prominent positions in the musical, legal and media worlds.
We strive to dedicate all our services here to the glory of God. Our music is not an
end in itself but part of a larger whole. We seek not only to inspire and to console,
but also to challenge. In selecting music there are many considerations, for example
drawing on the readings for the day and marking the feasts of the Church year, as well
as paying heed to the volume of music to be learnt by the Choir in any particular week.
At the same time, we hope that the music sung in this Chapel represents not only
something of the extraordinary heritage of which we are the current proud custodians
through pieces written by former or current Clerks, Choristers, Informatores and
Organ Scholars (indicated by the symbol M next to the name of a composer), but also
the richness and diversity of sacred music from across the Christian world in that
period, and particularly in the last half-century, when more music by women
composers and lesser-known works from the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries have
entered the repertoire. Living composers are indicated by the presence of their first
name in addition to their surname.
We hope that you will return to this place regularly, but, even if you happen upon
just one day in the life of a Chapel and Choir that has sung daily services for over five
centuries, we hope that, in the singing and in the silence, in the spoken word and in
the beauty of this house of God, you will feel that greater presence, and be blessed by
the peace that passes all understanding.

MARK WILLIAMS                                                    ANDREW BOWYER
Informator Choristarum                                               Dean of Divinity

                                 Choral Services
     Services are normally sung by the College Choir made up of boy Choristers,
educated at Magdalen College School, and male and female Academical Clerks, who
are ordinarily students at the College. Evensong on Saturdays is normally sung by the
   Consort of Voices, in which sopranos join the Academical Clerks. Tuesdays are
  usually sung by the Choristers alone, and Fridays by the Academical Clerks alone.
  Compline on Tuesdays is usually sung by two cantors drawn from the Academical
   Clerks and Consort of Voices. Details are given in the Music List when services
  depart from this pattern, such as during the school half-term holiday (24th to 29th
   May). At Festal Evensongs marking saintsÕ days and major feasts of the ChurchÕs
   year, a seasonal antiphon is sung. On Sundays, at the Eucharist, there is a short
homily of approximately five minutes, and at Evensong (in some weeks) a sermon of
approximately ten minutes. On Sundays, the Eucharist is followed by a simple lunch
in the College Hall, and Evensong by drinks in the Antechapel. Tuesday Compline is
                         followed by drinks in the Antechapel.

                                  Organ Music
  Organ music plays an integral part in our liturgy. The Assistant Organist, Drew
   Sellis, and Organ Scholars, Henry Morris and Sea-am Thompson, play for the
     majority of the services in Chapel, assisted occasionally by the Informator
    Choristarum & Organist, Mark Williams, who ordinarily directs the Choir.
  Details of the organ, by Hermann Eule Orgelbau, can be found on the College
           website, and details of recitals are given on the following pages.

                      Supporting the Choir and Chapel
The Chapel and Choir are grateful for the generous support of the College for their
 core activities, but look to benefactors to support additional activities, including
  recordings, concerts and tours. If you are interested in joining the Friends of
 Magdalen College Choir, please contact the Informator Choristarum, through the
Choir Administrator at choir@magd.ox.ac.uk, or collect a leaflet from the Chapel.

                           Membership of the Choir
   The Informator Choristarum, Mark Williams, is always pleased to hear from
   prospective applicants for Choristerships, Academical Clerkships and Organ
Scholarships. To arrange an informal meeting, please contact choir@magd.ox.ac.uk
