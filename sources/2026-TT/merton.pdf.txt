                   Merton College Chapel

Merton College was founded by Walter de Merton in 1264. Worship
has been oﬀered in the College Chapel for over seven hundred
years. Today the Chapel is home to a lively and diverse community
which continues this faithful oﬀering of worship with daily services
during term. Visitors are welcome to join members of the College
at all our services.

The Chapel is part of the Church of England, within the worldwide
Anglican Communion, and the Archbishop of Canterbury is the
College’s Visitor. All those who are communicant members of their
own church are welcome to receive Holy Communion with us.

A Choral Foundation was established at Merton in 2008. The
College Choir, directed by Benjamin Nicholas, sings on Sundays,
Tuesdays and Thursdays. Its patron is Peter Phillips, the founder
and director of The Tallis Scholars. Girl choristers from a variety of
local schools were admi ed to the Choral Foundation in 2016, and
normally sing Vespers on Mondays and Evensong on Wednesdays.

Members of College can purchase the College Choir and Choristers’
recordings and Benjamin Nicholas’ organ discs at a discounted
price from the Verger (jennifer.crompton@merton.ox.ac.uk). Copies
are also available from the Porters’ Lodge and the online shop on
the College website (www.merton.ox.ac.uk).

The Chapel supports a number of charities. Our collections this
term will be given to Embrace the Middle East, a Christian charity
working with local partners to support vulnerable and
marginalised communities through education, healthcare, and
community development across the region.
                                                      Justin White
