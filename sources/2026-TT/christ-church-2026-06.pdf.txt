The Choral Foundation of Christ Church:
The Cathedral Choir
When Henry VIII founded Christ Church in 1546 he made
provision for the education of a number of boy choristers and a
schoolmaster. From these historic roots has grown the current
Christ Church Cathedral School, of about 150 pupils aged three to
13, housed in premises built by Dean Liddell (father of Lewis
Carroll’s ‘Alice.’) The cathedral choristers are dedicated to their
daily music-making, & to the transformational experience that
singing in one of the world’s most famous choirs brings.
The Lay and Academical Clerks, as part of the choral foundation
at Christ Church, sing with the boy choristers, and with
Frideswide Voices, at services throughout the year. The Lay Clerks
are professional singers, whilst the Academical Clerks are
undergraduates reading for a degree at the University of Oxford.
The Organist (Director of Music), Peter Holder, and Christ Church
Cathedral School welcome enquiries at any time about being a
chorister at Christ Church. Please contact the School Registrar
on 01865 242561 or email registrar@cccs.org.uk : they will be
pleased to arrange a tour of the School and Boarding House or
an informal voice trial. www.cccs.org.uk

Frideswide Voices
Frideswide Voices was the first Anglican choir created
specifically for girls of chorister age in Oxford, and was originally
formed in October 2014. While they have always had significant
links with Christ Church, they became part of the Foundation in
September 2019. The girls are drawn from more than 25
different schools across the city and county. They sing Evensong
in the Cathedral on most Wednesdays during term-time, as well
as singing other services throughout the year.
The Associate Choral Director, Alexander Turner, welcomes
enquiries about choristerships at any time. Please email
alexander.turner@chch.ox.ac.uk for further information.
chch.ox.ac.uk/frideswidevoices
Unless stated otherwise, all choral services will be sung by
members of the Choral Foundation.
Other Choirs at Christ Church:
The Cathedral Singers of Christ Church
The Cathedral Singers is the Cathedral’s adult mixed-voice choir,
singing when the other choirs are away or on holiday. Under
its own professional director and organist, the choir operates a
squad system in order to provide choral music for up to 100
services a year. We welcome new singers, who should be
confident readers of music. For more information, please email
their director at susie.hill@chch.ox.ac.uk
www.cathedralsingers.org.uk

The College Choir of Christ Church
The College Choir is composed of Junior Members of Christ
Church and colleagues from across the University. As such, it is
the only student-run choir at Oxford to sing in Christ Church
Cathedral regularly. In addition to regular services of choral
Evensong, Compline, and Eucharist in the Cathedral, the College
Choir also sings in local churches and the Oxfordshire
community.
The ensemble prides itself on maintaining a high standard of
singing in a relaxed, non-auditioned, and friendly atmosphere. If
you are an Oxford University student interested in singing with us,
please feel free to be in touch at chchcollegechoir@gmail.com
www.chch.ox.ac.uk/choirs/college-choir
Visiting Choirs
When our own choirs are not in residence, it is a delight to
welcome visitors from around the country and beyond. This may be
school or university choirs, amateur or professional groups, and they
may be with us for a single service, a few days or a whole week.
If your choir would like to organise a residency at Christ Church,
please get in touch with us at visitingchoirs@chch.ox.ac.uk

us on Saturday 20 June for the centrepiece concert of the choir’s

the time of Henry VIIIth by Christ Church’s first choirmaster, John
Taverner, to the world premiere of a new cantata by our Composer in
Residence, Nico Muhly: The Shadow of the Sun. Buy your tickets now,
including a discounted rate for students:

Support Your Cathedral
Your support makes a lasting difference to Christ Church Cathedral.
For centuries, this Cathedral has sustained a ministry of worship,
outreach, pilgrimage and community at the heart of the Diocese.
Today, your gift – whether through a donation or a legacy – helps
preserve our historic fabric, uphold our tradition of musical excellence,
and develop our ministry among people across the Diocese and
beyond. Please consider how you might help secure the Cathedral’s
witness for generations to come. If you would like to become more
involved or hear more about how you can support the Cathedral,
please contact: Thomas Ridenour, Development Manager (Cathedral &
Cathedral Music), thomas.ridenour@chch.ox.ac.uk

Safeguarding
If you have any concerns about the safety of a child or vulnerable adult
in or around Christ Church Cathedral, please let us know immediately.
Please speak to any member of the clergy or verger team on duty, or
contact the safeguarding team at:
cathedral.safeguarding@chch.ox.ac.uk

Evacuation Procedure
In the unlikely event of an emergency, please follow the instructions
of the Vergers, and calmly make your way out through the West
(Main) door – or alternative exit if necessary – and move away from the
building.
