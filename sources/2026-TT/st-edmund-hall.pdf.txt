The Chapel
Prayer has been offered on the site of St Edmund
Hall for over a thousand years, and the Chapel
has stood for nearly four centuries as a place for
Christian worship. It is open to all members of the
Hall. The Chaplain is available as a pastoral adviser
to those of any faith or none, and can also offer
information about Christian and other faith groups
at the Hall or around the city. If you would like to
explore the Christian faith, or to seek Baptism or
Confirmation, please also contact the chaplain.




The Choir
The College Choir is made up of about twenty-
four singers, including eight choral scholars
and two organ scholars. They sing Evensong
on Sundays and Compline on Thursdays
during term, as well other special occasions and
concerts. Anyone interested in joining is warmly
encouraged to contact the Director of Music.
